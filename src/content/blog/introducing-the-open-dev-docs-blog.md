The courses on Open Dev Docs are structured, sequential, and built to be read start to finish. The blog is for everything that does not fit that shape.

## What goes here

- Short explanations of a single concept that does not warrant a full course topic
- Notes on new releases in the languages and frameworks covered by the courses
- Behind-the-scenes posts about how the site itself is built
- Corrections, changelogs, and roadmap updates

## How posts get published

Every post is a markdown file in `src/content/blog/`. Posts are written and edited through the CMS at `/admin`, which commits the file to the repository. The site rebuilds and deploys from that commit automatically.

A post with **Draft** switched on stays out of the published site, so work in progress can be saved safely without going live.

## What to expect next

Course content stays the priority, so posts here are occasional rather than scheduled. Everything published is free, and corrections are always welcome.
